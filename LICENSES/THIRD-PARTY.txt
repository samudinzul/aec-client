    License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2020-present Silero Team

12. FireRed Stream-VAD (alternate voice-gate detector, experimental)
    Upstream: FireRedTeam/FireRedVAD (Xiaohongshu, Apache-2.0),
    streaming variant (~0.6M params, DFSMN, 16 kHz, 10 ms frames).
    ONNX export via leospark/FireRedVAD-Engineering (MIT), validated
    <1.19e-7 vs PyTorch by the exporter.
    Files (download on demand, not committed):
      models/model_with_caches.onnx (sha256 19fe5c62…)
      models/model_with_caches.onnx.data (sha256 17cbbe1a…)
      models/firered_cmvn.bin (80 global means + 80 inverse stds,
        f32LE, generated from the official cmvn.ark with kaldiio and
        cross-checked against the safetensors vectors to ~1e-7;
        parity re-verified: fbank meanabs 0.0025, probs meanabs 5.4e-5
        vs golden_stream.safetensors)

13. ECAPA speaker embedding (owner-only voice gate, experimental)
    Upstream: vedk00/ecapa-voxceleb-speaker-embedding-onnx (Apache-2.0,
    mirror of speechbrain/spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb, 83 MB, 192-d).
    Silero/FireRed keeps timing; ECAPA verifies identity ~1 Hz on a
    worker thread. Voiceprint never leaves the PC
    (models/voiceprint.bin, untracked, not shipped).
    File (download on demand, not committed):
      models/ecapa-speaker-v1.onnx (sha256 f46380bb…)
