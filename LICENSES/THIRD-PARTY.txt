12. ECAPA speaker embedding (owner-only voice gate, experimental)
    Upstream: vedk00/ecapa-voxceleb-speaker-embedding-onnx (Apache-2.0,
    mirror of speechbrain/spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb, 83 MB, 192-d).
    The Silero gate keeps timing; ECAPA verifies identity ~1 Hz on a
    worker thread. Voiceprint never leaves the PC
    (models/voiceprint.bin, untracked, not shipped).
    File (download on demand, not committed):
      models/ecapa-speaker-v1.onnx (sha256 f46380bb…)

13. DEC baseline residual-echo cleanup (experimental)
    Upstream: microsoft/AEC-Challenge baseline/icassp2022 (MIT,
    5.2 MB, 16 kHz, 20 ms window / 10 ms hop, 2xGRU-322 mask).
    Runs after AEC3 as an opt-in polisher
    (AEC3 output + speaker reference in, masked output out).
    File (committed, ships only if promoted from experimental):
      models/dec-baseline-icassp2022.onnx (sha256 4436ee4f…)
